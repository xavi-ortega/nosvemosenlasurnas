## Meaning and usefulness loop

Target 80 distinct questions and release at least 60 supported questions across ten or more topics. Never pad or invent positions to reach the count. Each question asks one concrete everyday decision, normally in 12-25 Spanish words; brief optional neutral help explains necessary terms without inventing outcomes or advertising an answer. Preserve source conditions even when that requires a slightly longer sentence. No unexplained acronyms, double negation, bundled proposals or analyst-level prerequisites.

For every question state what agreement with its exact proposition means. +2 is clear agreement, -2 clear opposition; qualified +/-1 and explicitly evidenced intermediate 0 need a rationale. Silence, ambiguity and conflict are never neutral. Reopen full source context for each scored mapping and retain an independent calibration record. Whole-law repeal does not imply rejection of every provision.

Trace visible ordered labels (-2,-1,0,1,2), selected value, stored answer, question polarity, cited party value, arithmetic contribution and the displayed explanation. The reported rent-cap example is mandatory: strongly disagree versus supporting rent caps must be a disagreement. A party may still have the highest total overlap from other questions, so show those relationships separately. Do not blindly invert the scoring formula; the initial isolated arithmetic test was correct, while radio ordering was demonstrably wrong. Full browser reproduction and real-source semantic verification remain required.

Rank only a disclosed fixed evidence-qualified subset of eligible parties using identical scored questions and weights; preserve all other eligible candidacies and missing evidence. No different-set partial-score leaderboard, forced winner or probability/voting instruction. Results should explain practical agreements, disagreements and what could not be established, with original citations in optional disclosures.
