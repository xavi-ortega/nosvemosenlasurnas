# Community conduct

Welcome people of all backgrounds, ages, abilities, languages and political views. Discuss evidence and implementation with respect. Critique claims and decisions without attacking people. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing and repeated personal abuse are unacceptable.

This is a neutral information project. Contributions must not introduce party promotion, pressure people to disclose political preferences or misrepresent source material. Disagreement is expected; support factual assertions with verifiable sources and distinguish evidence from interpretation.

Maintainers may remove abusive content, request revisions, restrict participation or exclude repeated offenders. They should explain decisions, consider context and conflicts of interest, and protect reporters' privacy.

Before opening public contributions, the project owner must appoint a conduct contact and publish a verified private reporting route. No contact has been invented in this scaffold. Do not post sensitive reports or identifying information in public issues.
